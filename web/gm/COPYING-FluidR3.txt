FluidR3_GM_drums_piano.sf2
==========================

The General MIDI drum kit and piano the music falls back on when the player has not added their
own gm.dls. It is trimmed from the Fluid (R3) General MIDI SoundFont by `cctool gmtrim`
(engine/tools/gm_trim.cpp): only the Standard drum kit (bank 128, preset 0) and the Yamaha Grand
Piano (bank 0, preset 0) are kept, each stereo pair is mixed to one mono sample, the samples are
resampled to 22,050 Hz, and modulators are left out.
